⌚ How to Choose the Best Smart Watch Under ₹2000

Before buying a smartwatch, compare important features such as display quality, battery life, fitness tracking, calling features, water resistance and compatibility with your smartphone.

1. Display Quality

Look for a bright and clear display that is easy to read indoors and outdoors.

2. Battery Life

A good smartwatch should provide enough battery life for everyday use without requiring frequent charging.

3. Health & Fitness Features

Depending on your needs, consider features such as step tracking, heart-rate monitoring, sleep tracking and multiple sports modes.

4. Bluetooth Calling

If you want to answer calls from your wrist, check whether the smartwatch supports Bluetooth calling and has a built-in microphone and speaker.
